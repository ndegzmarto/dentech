About Us

Our Journey

DENTECH EXPERTISE PREMIER Vehicle Workshop, located in Ruiru, Kenya, is an automotive repair shop renowned for its certified master technicians. We are dedicated to ensuring the safety and reliability of our clients' vehicles through expert maintenance and repair services.

Our focus is on vehicle owners in Ruiru and the surrounding areas who value high-quality and trustworthy automotive services.

Dentech Expertise and Technical Training, founded in June 2024, aims to empower youth through technical education in automotive and fiberglass technology, with special attention to people with special needs including the deaf, orphans, and vulnerable individuals.

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision

We aim to help people with special needs reach their goals by creating opportunities for them to fulfill their dreams. We advocate for fair and equal treatment for all citizens.

  • Reduce the rate of youth unemployment
  • Make Kenya a future poverty-eradicated country
  • Reduce the rate of illiteracy in the country
  • Make training courses accessible to low-income families

Our Mission

We are committed to creating a positive impact in our community through:

  • Creating awareness and empowering youth's self-independence
  • Providing affordable technical training courses in a conducive environment
  • Adopting flexible payment options for low-income families
  • Building a community of skilled professionals who can contribute to Kenya's growth

Our Focus Groups

We focus on empowering diverse groups who often face significant challenges and require support:

Deaf Community

We address communication barriers, accessibility issues, and fight against stigma and discrimination faced by deaf individuals through specialized training programs.

Orphans

We provide emotional support, educational opportunities, and financial assistance to orphans who face challenges with family support, social integration, and educational access.

Youth

We focus on providing quality education and career training for youth to help them navigate developmental challenges and reach their full potential.
